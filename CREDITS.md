## 1. NASA & International Mission Attribution

This project is built directly upon the extraordinary engineering, cartographic, and scientific achievements of NASA’s Mars 2020 Perseverance mission, the USGS Astrogeology Science Center, and the international Mars exploration community.

### Mission & Agency Leadership
- **NASA Mars Exploration Program & Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL / Caltech):** Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover mission design, surface operations, traverse navigation, and science leadership.
- **USGS Astrogeology Science Center (Flagstaff, Arizona):** High-resolution Terrain Relative Navigation (TRN) orthorectified image mosaics and Digital Terrain Models (DTMs) of Jezero Crater.
- **European Space Agency (ESA):** Mars Express HRSC orbital imagery and regional context.
- **NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Geosciences Node:** Open-access archival of rover localization, drive tracks, and instrument telemetry.

## 2. Rover Science Instrument Teams

We gratefully acknowledge the scientists and engineers operating Perseverance's suite of in-situ instruments whose data informs every target assessment in JEI:

- **PIXL Team (Planetary Instrument for X-ray Lithochemistry):** NASA JPL. Fine-scale elemental chemistry and micro-spatial map identification of iron phosphate (vivianite) and iron sulfide (greigite) reaction rims.
- **SHERLOC Team (Scanning Habitable Environments with Raman & Luminescence for Organics & Chemicals):** NASA JPL & Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL). Deep-UV Raman and fluorescence detection of organic molecules and associated minerals.
- **Mastcam-Z Team:** Arizona State University & Malin Space Science Systems (MSSS). Multispectral, stereoscopic panoramic imaging and ground-level documentation of rock textures.
- **SuperCam Team:** Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL), CNES, and IRAP France. Laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS), remote Raman, and infrared reflectance spectroscopy.
- **RIMFAX Team (Radar Imager for Mars' Subsurface Experiment):** University of Oslo & Forsvarets forskningsinstitutt (FFI), Norway. Ground-penetrating radar profiling of crater floor stratigraphy.
- **MEDA Team (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer):** Centro de Astrobiología (CAB / CSIC-INTA), Spain. Continuous ambient pressure, temperature, wind, relative humidity, and dust monitoring.
